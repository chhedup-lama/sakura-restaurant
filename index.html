Our Story

One kitchen, three cuisines, zero compromises.

Sakura Family Restaurant brings Japanese, Korean and Chinese cooking together under one roof on the first floor of RS Plaza, Methibari — a calm, Japandi-styled room built for long, unhurried meals with people you like. Guests come back for the hand-rolled sushi and nigiri platters as often as they do for panko-fried chicken and a bowl of bibimbap.

Every plate is made to order, every table gets free Wi-Fi and parking is sorted downstairs — so the only thing left to plan is who's ordering the Big Sushi Platter.
